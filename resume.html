Research Preprint — Cybersecurity Workforce Training

Closing the Human Element Gap in Cybersecurity: A Continuous-Microlearning Intelligence Pipeline and the Jumpmaster Training Model

Synthesizes peer-reviewed literature on human factors, microlearning, and continuous reinforcement to argue that the cybersecurity stack is overinvested in reactive technology relative to workforce risk reduction. Presents the CyberSense.Solutions operating model and the Jumpmaster Training Model — a daily, deliberate, role-realistic awareness framework adapted from elite military airborne operations — supported by proof-of-concept data from 103 consecutive briefing editions (35.7% impression growth, 51.9% reach growth over 135 days). Proposes a four-component controlled evaluation protocol using the HAIS-Q instrument.
