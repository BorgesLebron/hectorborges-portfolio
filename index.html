CyberSense (Insights & Projects)

Cyber Brief: The Human Element in Zero Trust

A recent industry study found that over 70% of successful cyberattacks began with human error — not technical flaws. Organizations often invest millions in Zero Trust architecture but forget the “zero trust” mindset starts with people.

Zero Trust means never assume safety, even in familiar digital spaces. Just as a pilot runs checklists before every flight, we must treat every email, link, and login as a new preflight inspection.

CyberSense takeaway: Treat verification as second nature, not suspicion. Trust is earned — even by your inbox.

Training Byte: The 3-Second Cyber Pause

Before opening any link, attachment, or message, pause for three seconds and ask:

  1. Do I recognize the sender?
  2. Is the request typical for them?
  3. Could this have been sent another way?

Three seconds can prevent three days of downtime.
